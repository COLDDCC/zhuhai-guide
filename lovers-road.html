Lovers' Road & Fisher Girl Statue: the Walk Every First-Timer Should Do

The 28km coastal path that defines Zhuhai. I've walked nearly every section of it — here's how to see the best part without wasting time.

I've lived in Zhuhai for [X] years, and the Fisher Girl Statue is my most-photographed spot in the city. Not because it's the biggest landmark — it isn't — but because it sits right where the city meets the sea. Lovers' Road stretches 28km along the coast and it's good in every season. Get it right once, and you've seen the real Zhuhai.

Last reviewed: today

Why Come Here — 3 Reasons

1. The landmark & its story

  • The Fisher Girl statue narrates Zhuhai's founding legend — the sea goddess who fell in love with a local fisherman and stayed onshore. It's the city's symbol, and the plaza fills up at sunset.
  • Best photo angles (my picks): from the seawall to her left you get the statue + sea + skyline; at 6pm the golden light wraps the silhouette.
  • Instagram hotspot — expect 10–30 photos being taken within your eye line, especially on weekends.

2. The walk & activities

  • Seaside boardwalk: paved all along the central 8–10km; flat, stroller and wheelchair friendly.
  • Cycling: rentals start near the iconic public bike-parking spots — about ¥15–20/hour or ¥20/day from the corner stands on the Xiangzhou side.
  • Sunrise & sunset: sunrise is calmest (locals' secret); sunset from the statue plaza is the classic photo. See my timing analysis in Daily Updates.

3. Food & services on the way

  • Roadside cafés along the Xiangzhou stretch — decent iced coffee and dessert stops with sea views.
  • Seafood restaurants cluster toward Wanzai Street, a 5-minute drive from the coast (see the seafood guide).
  • Public washrooms at roughly 1km intervals along the central section; the statue plaza has the most kept-up one.

Practical Guide — From My Own Experience

AvoidWeekend afternoons and the noon sun; both punish.

How to get there

  • Metro: Line 1 / Line 3 to Xiangzhou Port station — it's effectively the entrance. From the exits it's a 3–5 minute walk to the coast.
  • Bus: Routes 9, 99 and 观光02 all trace the coastline; get off at Lovers' Road stop.
  • Taxi / Didi: from downtown Zhuhai, about ¥15–30. Ask for "情人路" — every driver knows it.

Bike rental

  • Where: shared blue e-bikes (typical ¥3/30min) plus privatization rentals near the metro exits (~¥20/day).
  • What suits: for a flat coastal roll, any bike; the shared e-bikes handle the gentle hills best.
  • Round trip: an 8km out-and-back is doable in ~1.5 relaxed hours.

Safety notes

  • Sunscreen even in autumn — UV off the water is sneaky.
  • Lights along the main boardwalk are fine until late; the far ends get darker, so turn back by 9–10pm.
  • Parking: use the underground lot at Xiangzhou Port (¥5/hour); street parking on beaches fills fast.

My Recommended Day Trip (One Day / Half Day)

  • 10:00 Start at Xiangzhou Port metro station.
  • 10:20 Reach the Lovers' Road entrance.
  • 10:30–12:00 Ride the coast, stop at the beach for photos.
  • 12:30 Seafood noodle lunch at a small spot along the road (~¥25–40).
  • 14:00–16:00 Walk the front section, catch the daylight photos.
  • 17:00 Fisher Girl Statue at golden hour.
  • 18:00 Head back downtown for dinner (or straight to Wanzai Street).

Local Secrets I Only Learned After Months

  • Late night: after 11pm the coast empties out — the boardwalk feels private, and it's beautiful under streetlights.
  • Typhoon season (May–Oct): the boardwalk closes when warnings hit level 3; check the sky and the update log before planning around storms.
  • Spring Festival: the statue plaza can be shoulder-to-shoulder during holiday evenings — go at 7am if you want the plaza to yourself.
  • Clearest water: the few months after heavy rain in early spring look clearest, though few people actually swim here.
