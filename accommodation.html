Where to Stay in Zhuhai — the 3 Areas That Matter

Zhuhai isn't big, but picking the wrong neighborhood can sink your trip. Based on real conditions, here's how I split the city into three sensible zones — and which one fits you.

1. Gongbei — The Hub

Location: Zhuhai's busiest commercial center, right on the Macau border.

Good for: shopping, eating, nightlife; people crossing between Macau and Zhuhai several times; first-timers who want the heart of the city.

Avoid if: you want a quiet seaside retreat, or you're sensitive to noise at night.

Hotels

  • Luxury: King Zhuhai (5-star, sea views on the upper floors)
  • Mid-range: Gongbei Plaza Hotel (4-star, attached mall)
  • Budget: guesthouses at RMB 150–300/night around the port area

Key distances

  • Macau border gate: ~1km (15-min walk)
  • Lovers' Road: ~5km (15-min metro)
  • Zhuhai airport: ~15km (30-min taxi)

Food & transit

  • Underground food city at Gongbei for Cantonese; Haikou Road food street near the port for seafood.
  • Metro Line 1 passes through; densest bus network; cheapest taxis in town.
  • Typical price: RMB 300–800/night (3–5 star).

2. Xiangzhou — The Coast

Location: the coastal district holding Lovers' Road and Fisher Girl Statue.

Good for: sea views, boardwalk mornings, cycling; couples and families who want calm.

Avoid if: you want nightlife, or arrive very late — many shops close by 9pm.

Hotels

  • Luxury: Sheraton Zhuhai (4-star, beachfront on Lovers' Road)
  • Mid-range: Zhuhai Jingwan Hotel
  • Homestays: plenty of Airbnbs, RMB 200–500/night

Key distances

  • Lovers' Road: ~1km (walk)
  • Fisher Girl Statue: ~2km (bike)
  • Grand Theatre: ~3km (taxi)

Food & transit

  • Coastal cafés along Lovers' Road; seafood stalls at Xiangzhou Port; night market Thu–Fri.
  • Less central but metro-covered; taxis/Didi are easiest.
  • Typical price: RMB 250–600/night.

3. Hengqin — The New Development

Location: Zhuhai's newest district, the Macau/Zhuhai cooperation zone.

Good for: Chimelong Ocean Kingdom families; new-hotel fans; quick Macau arrivals.

Avoid if: you want traditional Zhuhai, or you're budget-tight — this district runs pricey.

Hotels

  • Top: Chimelong Hotel (themed, inside the park zone)
  • High-end: Alila Dong Ao Island resort
  • Mid-high: Pullman, Novotel

Key distances

  • Chimelong Ocean Kingdom: ~0.5km
  • Macau crossing: ~2km
  • Other sights: farther out

Food & transit

  • Park restaurants, a new HK-Macau-Taiwan food street, and upscale dining.
  • Typical price: RMB 600–2000/night.

My Personal Recommendations by Trip Type

  • First visit, 2 days only: stay in Gongbei. Lovers' Road in the morning, Macau in the afternoon, night market at night.
  • Chimelong + nature: Hengqin — the park is here and the coast is reachable.
  • One week, local life: Xiangzhou — rent a homestay, cycle daily, eat at the port stalls each night.
  • Traveling with family: Hengqin (Chimelong) or Xiangzhou (beach + safety).
