Chimelong Ocean Kingdom — My No-Wasted-Time Route

Zhuhai's biggest attraction, and if you're traveling with family, it's basically mandatory. This is my field-tested plan: the tickets, the queues, and the order that saves you hours.

Last reviewed: today

Today: 10% off family packages this weekend — same price as the Alipay deal.

Why It's Worth It — 3 Reasons

  • World-class marine exhibits — including the giant acrylic-dome aquarium (largest of its kind) and daily shows.
  • Big rides — the parrot coaster is among the tallest and fastest of its type; the "ocean drift" runs all weather.
  • Fresh-seafood dining — premium restaurants inside, some with dolphin-view seating.

Getting There — Fastest Options

  • Rail (cheapest): ¥11, 28 min. Take the Zhuhai–Zhuhai Airport intercity rail from Zhuhai Railway Station (Gongbei) to Zhuhai Changlong Station — the park entrance is a short walk from the exit.
  • Taxi / Didi (fastest): ~¥50–80, 25 min outside rush. Weekends add time.
  • Bus: ~¥3–5, ~60 min.

My habit: rail Mon–Fri, taxi on weekends — the extra yuan buys an hour of park time.

Park Map — What's Where

Zone 1 — Aquarium & shows (a must, but not a rush)

  • The acrylic dome: look down, sharks swim overhead.
  • Dolphin show: 3 daily — 10am / 2pm / 6pm.
  • Budget 2–3 hours here.

Zone 2 — Rides (where queues build)

  • Parrot coaster — top-5 ranked globally.
  • Ocean drift — Asia's longest; runs in any weather.
  • Ferris wheel — park panoramas.
  • Line times: 1–2h per ride (3h+ in holiday peak).

Zone 3 — Family stage shows

  • Beluga show — gentle, kid-friendly.
  • Evening water-stunt show.

My Recommended Route (no wasted steps)

  • 7:00 Leave early to dodge traffic.
  • 10:00 Gates open — sprint to the parrot coaster (quietest window of the day).
  • 11:30 Ocean drift — opposite side, still short.
  • 13:00 Lunch (in-park or out at the entrance street).
  • 14:30 Aquarium + dolphin show.
  • 17:00 Break / shopping.
  • 18:00 Dinner.
  • 19:00 Water-stunt show.
  • 20:30 Ferris wheel at sunset.
  • 21:00 Leave.

Only have half a day? Pick two of three: parrot coaster, dolphin show, ocean drift.

Food — In-Park Is Expensive, Here's My Workaround

  • In-park restaurants: ¥80–150 / person — about 3× normal Zhuhai pricing, with 30–45 min lunch queues.
  • My move: buy breakfast at a nearby convenience store (¥20), eat a cheap lunch outside the gate (the entrance street is ~50% cheaper), or pack a picnic — there's a designated area.

Special Needs & Practical

  • Parking: ¥30/day at the park lot.
  • Babies: nursing rooms and stroller rentals (¥50/day).
  • Wheelchairs: rentals available at the same price.
  • Rain: most rides are roofed; it's business as usual.

FAQ

How old/big to ride the parrot coaster?

About 1.4m tall or age ~8 at a minimum.

Is a full day too tiring?

It can be — that's why the route above bakes in rest stops and sits down for shows.

What should I bring?

Sunscreen, water, a power bank, and properly broken-in shoes.

I lost something inside?

Ask at guest services — they keep a Lost & Found.

Dates to Watch

  • Avoid Spring Festival & National Day: 10× crowds, pricier tickets.
  • Summer (Jul–Aug): hot and packed.
  • Best seasons: March and October — shorter lines, mild weather.
