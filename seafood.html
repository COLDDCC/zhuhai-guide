Best Seafood in Zhuhai — Wanzai Street & More

Zhuhai's seafood isn't as famous as Guangzhou's — which is exactly why it's cheaper and fresher here. This guide is the result of many, many dinners.

1. Wanzai Seafood Street — The Local Choice

Location: central Xiangzhou district, a 5-minute drive from Lovers' Road.

  • A whole street of open-air dai pai dongs.
  • You pick live fish / shrimp / shellfish → hand it to a stall → they cook it on the spot.
  • Very cheap, very fresh.
  • Peak crowd 7–10pm.

Price reference (per jin / 500g)

  • Grouper: RMB 80–120 / jin
  • Prawns: RMB 60–100 / jin
  • Clams & shellfish: RMB 30–50 / jin
  • Cooking charge (grill / stir-fry / steam): RMB 30–50 / dish
  • Average 2-person meal: RMB 200–400

My must-orders

  • Steamed grouper — the freshest test of anything in the tank.
  • Garlic prawns
  • Stir-fried razor clams — a Zhuhai specialty.
  • Boiled crab — at its sweetest in winter.

Getting there

  • Tip: give drivers "万载海鲜街".
  • Metro Line 3 → Wanzai stop, ~8-min walk.
  • From downtown: RMB 15–25 taxi.

Hours

  • From 3pm (prep), peak at 6pm, winding down ~11pm. Open Sundays too.

3. Cantonese Tea Houses (Dim Sum)

  • Gongbei has 2–3 long-standing houses.
  • Go 7–11am for the widest cart selection.
  • The carts roll to your table — beckon what you want.
  • Per person: RMB 50–80.
  • Order first: shrimp dumplings (har gow), shaomai, and chicken feet.

4. Street Snacks (Cheapest)

  • Gongbei: claypot rice street (~¥5 a bowl).
  • Xiangzhou: the cold-noodle stand (~¥3).
  • Night markets (Thu–Fri): assorted snacks, ¥5–15.

Dining Etiquette & Local Notes

  • Don't waste food — Cantonese culture takes it seriously.
  • Leave the table tidy; or just flag the server for help.
  • Tipping is rare — rounding up to 1–2 yuan is fine.
  • WeChat / Alipay accepted almost everywhere; cash largely optional.

Zhuhai Food Specialties Worth Buying

  • Zhuhai salted fish — best bought at the wet market stalls; asking "对嘴咸鱼" gets you the good ones.
  • Oyster sauce — the well-known brands are premium; the market versions are the freshest.
  • Spring rolls — the most traditional versions come from the small teahouses in Gongbei.
