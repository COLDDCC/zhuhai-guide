How to Get to Zhuhai from Hong Kong, Macau & Shenzhen

Zhuhai is a transport hub by design — Hong Kong, Macau, and Shenzhen are all within about an hour. This guide picks your cheapest, fastest, or most comfortable route, step by step.

Option 1 — From Hong Kong: Golden Bus (Most Popular)

This is how I bring friends over. Direct, one seat, straight across the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge.

What it is

The cross-border coaches run direct from Hong Kong International Airport and China/HK ferry terminals, crossing the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge — the world's longest sea bridge. Land in Zhuhai downtown (~45 min driving) with one transfer of buses at the border.

The actual flow

  • After landing follow "Mainland Coach" signs; Golden Bus desks sit near exits B & C.
  • Three checks: HK departure → the bridge → Chinese mainland entry at Zhuhai port. Same-day, one-time immigration on the bus side.
  • Boarding: coaches leave every 20–30 min; buy same-day at the desk or the app.
  • On board: USB charging ports yes, Wi-Fi often patchy mid-bridge — a mainland SIM helps (see below).

Cost

  • HK$120 / adult (2026 fare). Under-3s free; 3–11 half price.
  • Includes your seat only — meals not included, but port food courts exist.

My experience

  • Real door-to-door (HK Airport → Zhuhai downtown): ~1.5h including queues on a normal weekday, closer 2h on weekends.
  • Border wait: 15–40 min typical. Friday evening / Sunday return spikes hard.
  • You get off at the Zhuhai main bus station / port — 20 min taxi to Xiangzhou hotels, 10 min to Gongbei.

Pro tips

  • Book next-day at "Golden Bus" official app / WeChat mini-program to lock the HK$120 rate.
  • Avoid Fri 16:00–20:00 and Sun afternoon returns.
  • Bags: free up to 2 pieces for the under-carriage; oversized = gate charge.
  • Seasick-prone pals: sit upper deck, front rows, face forward.

Option 2 — From Hong Kong: Ferry (Scenic)

  • Depart HK from China Ferry Terminal (Tsim Sha Tsui) or HK Airport SkyPier.
  • ~70 min, ~HK$180; arriving at Zhuhai Jiuzhou Port — right at the coast near Lovers' Road.
  • Better views of the bay islands than the bridge, fewer border queues.
  • Rougher in typhoon season (May–Oct) — check cancellations before planning a fixed evening.

Option 3 — From Macau: Walk Across at Gongbei (Simplest)

This is my favorite "wow, that easy?" moment for guests. You can literally walk from Macau to Zhuhai.

Why it's the easiest

  • No ticket, no vehicle, no connection — just walk across the covered bridge.
  • Only need your passport (or the usual mainland/Macau ID you'd cross with).

Step by step

  • Walk from anywhere central Macau to the "Border Gate" (Portas do Cerco). All taxis/buses know it.
  • Queue at Macau exit — 10–30 min typical; mornings shortest.
  • Cross the footbridge into Zhuhai's Gongbei BGB building; pass mainland entry.
  • You're in Gongbei — Zhuhai's commercial center. Metro Line 1 & taxis sit right outside.

Watch out

  • Gongbei opens daily, typically 06:00–01:00; extended to 24h around holidays — confirm same-day in the update log.
  • Bag rules standard; bring nothing restricted and allow shoe-level security scans.
  • No vehicles walkable — taxis/Didi only on the Zhuhai side (¥10–15 downtown).

Option 4 — From Shenzhen: Ferry or HSR

Ferry (Shekou → Jiuzhou)

  • ~60 min, ~¥120, frequent sailings from Shekou Port;
  • Stop at Zhuhai Jiuzhou Port — close to the coast, cafés, and Lovers' Road.

High-speed rail (my pick)

  • Shenzhen North / Futian → Zhuhai's main station: ~75 min, ~¥80 second-class.
  • Most comfortable option: reserved seats, luggage racks, punctual.
  • Book in-app (Railway 12306) up to 15 days ahead; weekends sell out early.

First Things to Do After You Arrive — My Advice

  • Get online (30 min max): eSIM from a major provider before you fly, or a SIM at the airport store (~¥30–50). Alipay / WeChat Pay cover almost everything — set them up with your home card early.
  • Download: WeChat, Alipay, 高德地图 (Amap) or 百度地图 (Baidu Maps), Didi.
  • Hotel or pickup: taxi stands are chaotic only at rush; Didi cars are cheaper and queueless.
  • If anything goes wrong — lost luggage, no room, locked out — we answer within 2 hours, 24/7.

Seasonal Notes

  • Typhoon season (May–Oct): bridge and ferries can pause; check alerts 24h before travel.
  • Spring Festival (late Jan–Feb): Gongbei queues and bridge traffic multiply; add an hour.
  • Best all-round: March–April and October–November, weekdays.

24h Emergency Help

FAQ

How much cash should I bring across the border?

¥200–300 RMB covers taxis, snacks, and small purchases; everything else is Alipay/WeChat. HK dollars are less useful on the mainland — exchange early if you're coming from HK/Macau.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes with a valid mainland import inspection + paperwork; always confirm both sides before crossing. Ferries are stricter than the bridge.

I use a wheelchair — is it possible?

Yes. Bridge bus has accessible seats and staff; Gongbei walkway is ramped; HSR is wheelchair friendly. Tell the operator 48h ahead where possible.

What documents do kids need?

Children cross on their own passport or a visa-required country — check visa rules (Z visa-free and 72h transit apply to many nationals). Kids under 16 can't typically use e-gates.

Do I need a visa, and when?

Many nationalities get 30–60 day visa-free entry to China mainland (2026). 72-hour visa-free transit applies when continuing to a third country. Always confirm your exact nationality/date before travel.
